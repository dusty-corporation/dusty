Dusty 1.2.1 is a maintenance release of Dusty 1.2.0, for Windows and for Macs with Apple silicon. Private transcription that never leaves your computer.

## Downloads

| | File | Works on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Windows | `Dusty-1.2.1-Setup.exe` | Windows 10 22H2 or Windows 11, 64-bit Intel or AMD |
| Mac | `Dusty-1.2.1-macos-arm64.dmg` | A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 13.3 or later |

Check your download against `SHA256SUMS.txt` before opening it. Every character must match.

## Upgrading from Dusty 1.2.0 or 1.1.3

Your downloaded models, settings and transcripts are kept.

- **Windows:** run the new Setup. It replaces the installed Dusty.
- **Mac:** quit Dusty, drag the new Dusty to Applications and choose Replace.

The downloads are free and not signed with a paid certificate, so your computer may show the one-time warning again.

- **Windows:** if you see "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway. Setup does not need administrator rights.
- **Mac:** when macOS says Dusty was not opened, choose Done. Then open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway next to the Dusty message.

Never turn off your computer's security to install Dusty.

## What's new

Nothing changes for you in this version. Your transcripts, settings and saved queues are exactly as in Dusty 1.2.0. Dusty's source was reorganised internally, and every build is now checked against the files Dusty 1.2.0 wrote.

Models download only with your permission, during Setup on Windows or in Model Manager. Your recordings and transcripts are never uploaded.
