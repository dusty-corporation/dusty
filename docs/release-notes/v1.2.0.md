Dusty 1.2.0 adds optional time markers and marks on words that may be wrong, for Windows and for Macs with Apple silicon. Private transcription that never leaves your computer.

## Downloads

| | File | Works on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Windows | `Dusty-1.2.0-Setup.exe` | Windows 10 22H2 or Windows 11, 64-bit Intel or AMD |
| Mac | `Dusty-1.2.0-macos-arm64.dmg` | A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 13.3 or later |

Check your download against `SHA256SUMS.txt` before opening it. Every character must match.

## Upgrading from Dusty 1.1.3

Your downloaded models, settings and transcripts are kept.

- **Windows:** run the new Setup. It replaces the installed Dusty.
- **Mac:** quit Dusty, drag the new Dusty to Applications and choose Replace.

The downloads are free and not signed with a paid certificate, so your computer may show the one-time warning again.

- **Windows:** if you see "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway. Setup does not need administrator rights.
- **Mac:** when macOS says Dusty was not opened, choose Done. Then open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway next to the Dusty message.

Never turn off your computer's security to install Dusty.

## What's new

Both are in Options, under In Text and Word files. They are off until you turn them on, and change only Text and Word transcripts.

- **Time markers.** Adds the time on its own line about every few minutes, at an interval you choose from 0.5 to 120 minutes, such as 5 or 5.4. Each marker waits for the end of a sentence or a pause and shows the real time of the text after it. In Word the markers are headings, listed in the Navigation pane.
- **Mark words that may be wrong.** Adds [?] after words Dusty is unsure of, such as names, and highlights them in Word. Choose only very unsure words or also somewhat unsure ones. This is a hint, not a check: some marked words are right, and some mistakes are not marked, so still read the whole transcript.

Models download only with your permission, during Setup on Windows or in Model Manager. Your recordings and transcripts are never uploaded.
