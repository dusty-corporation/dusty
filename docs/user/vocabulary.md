# Vocabulary hints

In **Options...**, use **Vocabulary hints (optional)** for names and uncommon
terms you expect in a recording. Separate them with commas, for example:

`Dr. Rahimi, metformin, HbA1c`

Put the most important words first. Keep the list short; Dusty warns when it may
exceed the model's available space. Hints can help spelling, but do not force a
correct transcript. Review the result against the recording.

Hints stay on this computer and are passed directly to the local engine. They
are saved in settings and in unfinished-work state so you do not need to retype
them. They are not written to the diagnostic log or sent with model downloads.
Do not include identifying details you do not need. See [Privacy](../privacy.md)
for the local files that may contain them and the limits of deletion.
